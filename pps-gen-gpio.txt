Device-Tree Bindings for a PPS generated signal on GPIO

These properties describe a PPS (pulse-per-second) signal output 
through a GPIO pin.

Required properties:
- gpios: one PPS GPIO in the format described by ../gpio/gpio.txt

Optional properties:


Example:
	pps-gen {
		compatible = "pps-gen-gpio";
		gpios = <&gpio2 6 0>;
	};
